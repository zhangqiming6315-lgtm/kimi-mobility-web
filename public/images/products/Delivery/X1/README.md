- Motor Power: 3000W

## Publishing rules

- Do not publish a specific battery capacity, charging time, payload, IP rating, gradeability, net weight, battery brand, FOB price, MOQ, or container quantity until the final supplier configuration is confirmed.
- Use the Ofero-derived images as KIMI design-direction and marketing visuals.
- Do not use the unattractive supplier stock bike as the public-facing Delivery X1 product.
- Keep the KIMI master logo geometry unchanged.
- Maintain the graphite-gray body color with restrained Electric Green accents.
