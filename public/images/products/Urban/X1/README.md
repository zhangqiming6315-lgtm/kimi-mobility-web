## Safe public specifications

Use ranges until the supplier confirms the final export configuration:

- Motor: 2.2–3.0 kW rated
- Peak power: up to approximately 4.1 kW
- Battery platform: 72 V
- Top speed: up to 65–70 km/h, market and configuration dependent
- Urban range: up to 80–100 km, configuration and test-condition dependent
- Wheels: 12-inch wide tires
- Braking: front and rear disc brakes
- Seat: extended two-person seat
- Storage: practical under-seat compartment

Do not claim ABS, TCS, connected navigation, IP ratings or a specific storage volume until the OEM confirms those items in writing.
