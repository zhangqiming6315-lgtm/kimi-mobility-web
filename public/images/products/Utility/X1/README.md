# KIMI Utility X1 — Asset Pack V1

## Positioning

Utility X1 is a heavy-duty two-wheel electric utility motorcycle for service teams, commercial sites, field support and equipment transport. It is intentionally distinct from the step-through Urban X1 and the last-mile Delivery X1.

The vehicle architecture is based on the M1 reference in the JinJie Moto catalog: large motorcycle-format wheels, a long flat seat, exposed frame, dual rear shocks, a large modular rear rack and a protected central battery enclosure.

## Approved public specifications

- Rated motor power: 3000W
- Battery system: 72V
- Top speed: Up to 85 km/h
- Battery options: Multiple battery configurations
- Braking: Front and rear disc brakes
- Wheels: 18-inch front / 17-inch rear
- Cargo system: Modular heavy-duty rear platform

Do not publish an exact range or battery capacity until the final production configuration is confirmed. “Specifications may vary by market and configuration” should appear below the specification grid.
